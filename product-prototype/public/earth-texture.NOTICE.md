# Earth texture notice

`earth-at-night-2048.png` is stored locally so the homepage does not depend on a runtime image host.

- Source: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, “Earth At Night (WMS)”, ID 2916
- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2916/
- Downloaded asset: `earthatnight-2048.png` (2048 × 1024)
- Requested credit: data courtesy Marc Imhoff (NASA/GSFC) and Christopher Elvidge (NOAA/NGDC). Image by Craig Mayhew (NASA/GSFC) and Robert Simmon (NASA/GSFC).

The texture is used as a visual base only. Business nodes, routes, customers, states and outcomes displayed by the Qianhai prototype are explicitly simulated demo content.
